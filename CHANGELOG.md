## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-02

### Added

- The complete Wat Arun 3D riverside sanctuary: eleven-tier central prang, four
  satellite prangs, four mondops, ubosot with two yaksha guardians, six riverside salas,
  procedural river, boats, gardens, monks and gate.
- Five bilingual hotspots with narration, three lighting presets, first-person walking
  with collision and jumping, drone orbit, mini-map and touch controls.
- Synthesised river, chimes and footsteps, photo journal, `localStorage` autosave and
  portable JSON export/import.
- 28-check self-test at `?selftest=1`.
