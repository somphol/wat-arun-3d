    Field guide

    Walking the sanctuary

    First-person walk

    • WASD Walk; hold Shift to sprint
    • Space Jump; steps and ramps are walkable
    • Mouse Look around while pointer is locked
    • E Read the nearest hotspot
    • Esc Release the pointer

    Drone flyover

    • Drag Orbit the whole complex
    • Wheel Zoom from river level to the finial
    • Right-drag Pan the target
    • 1 Drone view preset over the river

    Sound & language

    • River, chimes and footsteps are synthesised live with Web Audio
    • Narration uses your device's English or Thai voice
    • EN / ไทย switches every label and description

    Tour journal

    • Visits and photo bookmarks autosave to this browser

    • Load restores a journal on any device
